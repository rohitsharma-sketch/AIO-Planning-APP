STR

STR Forecaster

STR in days = how long the minimum display stock lasts at the planned selling rate

← Landing

1 · Inputs

MENS, LADIES and KIDS by store, department and month. Minimum display stock (MDQ) = fixtures × qty per fixture, from the fixture plan; planned qty = the sales plan's qty (else planned sales ÷ last year's average selling price). STR in days = MDQ ÷ planned qty per day: 60 days means the minimum display stock lasts 60 days at the plan's selling rate - fewer days, faster sell-through. Band = the actual days rounded to the nearest 30, kept between 60 and 180 (54 → 60, 75 → 90, 104 → 90, 250 → 180); season-aware (default): peak months 30–90, off months 90–180, from the Listing - Delisting Analyser's seasonality. Only stores with every month in both files are compared. Click any figure to see how it is worked out.

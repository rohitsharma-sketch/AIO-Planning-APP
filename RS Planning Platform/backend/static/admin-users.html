What the roles mean

  • Planner - builds and edits plans: Calendar, AOP Forecaster, Buyer's Input Sheet, Sales Plan, Re-Aligner.
  • Buyer - works on the buyer's input for the plan cycles sent to them.
  • Reviewer - reviews submitted plan cycles and approves or sends them back.
  • Approver - gives the final approval on reviewed plan cycles.
  • Admin (with any role) - can open this page and manage people.
